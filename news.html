[2026-11-15] I implemented OpenMP parallelization for equation-of-state (EOS) evaluation and Jacobian assembly in serial TRGB, and integrated a GPU solver for matrix solving. The GPU solver achieved an 18.87× matrix-solving speedup, reducing matrix-solving time from 449.78 s to 23.84 s. Combining the GPU solver with OpenMP (8 threads) achieved an 8.61× overall speedup over the CPU baseline, reducing total elapsed time from 679.16 s to 78.84 s.
